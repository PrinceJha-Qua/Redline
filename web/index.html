Citations

    Each citation gets one of three verdicts.

    Fabricated
    Points into a volume Redline holds in full, and cannot be right.
    Verified
    The case exists. Then: good law, caution (it rests on an overruled case), or overruled.
    Unverifiable
    Outside Redline's data. Never called fake.

    Evidence

    Data: Caselaw Access Project, Harvard Law School Library; overrulings from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Graph: Relate by Neo4j, opened read-only. Verdicts come from graph queries, never from a language model.
