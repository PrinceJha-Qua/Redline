Fabricated

Page 1339 of 925 F.3d is inside J.D. v. Azar, 925 F.3d 1291 (D.C. Cir. 2019), which runs from page 1291 to 1349. No case named Varghese is in volume 925.

MATCH (:Volume {key:'F3d:925'})-[:CONTAINS]->(c:Case)
WHERE c.first_page <= 1339 AND c.last_page >= 1339
RETURN c.name, c.first_page, c.last_page

Three verdicts, each with its evidence

Fabricated

The citation cannot be right: the page belongs to another case, another case starts there, or the reporter series was never printed. Redline names the real case, and the real citation when the case exists elsewhere.

Verified

The case exists. Then good law, overruled, or caution: it relies on a case that was later overruled, and Redline shows the chain in the court's own words. Set the filing year and it judges by the law of that day.

Unverifiable

Outside Redline's data, or not provable from it. Never called fake. A wrong "fabricated" is worse than a missed one, so every doubt goes here.

One overruling moves thousands of cases

Chevron was overruled in 2024. Every decision that relied on it, and every decision that relied on those, now rests on law that no longer exists. Redline walks that chain in the graph.

For agents: find real authority instead of recalling it

An AI drafter invents citations because it cites from memory. Redline gives it a graph to cite from. The same tools serve Claude Code, any MCP client, the command line and the HTTP API.

find_authority

A proposition in, real cases out, each described in the words of the courts that cited it, with that circuit's own F.3d opinions. Overruled cases are ranked last.

check_citations

Every citation in a draft, with its verdict, its evidence, and for a fabricated one the real case with that name. Pass the filing date to judge by the law of that day.

what_if_overruled

Overrule a case inside a Relate transaction on the read-only store, re-check the brief with the same code, count who relied on it by circuit, and roll back. Relate refuses to commit it.

related_authority

Good-law cases courts cite together with yours, by co-citation, so an agent can widen its research without guessing.
